# Core idea

Stop someone from accidentally saying private or confidential information on a live call, such as a Zoom audio call, so the other person never hears it.

The audience is everyday workers who have NDAs and have to keep company information confidential.

The user supplies the buzz words. In the demo, they cannot say "tacos" while talking about making dinner. The app takes the microphone audio, turns it into words, finds the buzz words, locates those words in the audio, and censors them in place. The censored audio goes to the other person. The rest of the sentence passes through.

The call is tested in Zoom with no video, so only the audio path matters.
